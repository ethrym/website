Ethrym is a home for practical writing on two subjects: **product management** and **AI architecture**.

## What you'll find here

- **Posts** — short, regular essays from real work.
- **Books** — two books in progress, written in public.
- **Templates** — the documents I actually use, cleaned up for reuse.
- **Unvalidated reference architectures** — designs shared early, with their gaps stated openly.

## Why "unvalidated"?

Most reference architectures pretend to be finished. These don't. Each one lists what has and hasn't been tested, so you can decide how much to trust it.
