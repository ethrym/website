---
title: "Roadmaps: Separate Committed from Future"
date: 2026-10-08
category: Product Management
summary: The single change that makes a roadmap credible to executives and customers.
---

A roadmap mixes two kinds of statements: things you will ship, and things you hope to ship. Mixing them destroys trust.

## The rule

Put **committed** items — funded, staffed, scoped — in one lane. Put **future** items in another, and label them as direction, not promise.

## Why it works

1. Sales stops over-promising.
2. Executives can see what is really funded.
3. Customers learn that your commitments are reliable.
